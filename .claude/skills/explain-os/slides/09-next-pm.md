You own the top of the hierarchy — and the top is what makes everything below
it cheap.

**Your standing commands:**

- **`/plan-customer`** — once. Business goals, team, stack, repo index.
- **`/plan-product`** — once per product, with a techlead. Mission + roadmap
  are yours; tech-stack and architecture are theirs.
- **`/plan-epic`** — recurring. You write the product brief; Tech writes the
  tech plan. Either can go first.
- **`/shape-spec`** — recurring, as `PM` or `Both`. You can shape the *what*
  and leave the *how* pending for Tech.
- **`/project-status`** — read-only. The honest answer to "where are we?"
- **`/spec-changes`** — when reality moves. Edits the spec in place, with a
  Changelog row.

**Your best input is a meeting.** The `process-meeting` skill turns a recording
or transcript into a write-up under `meetings/` that ends in an ordered *what to
build next* — which feeds straight into `/plan-epic`.

**Where to leave a gap:** a section you can't answer yet stays as
`_Pending — rerun /shape-spec as Tech to fill._` That marker is a handoff, not
a failure. Leave it rather than guessing.
