# Account & Wishlist

> Product: ../../mission.md
> Status: planning
> Created: 2026-10-05 · Roles planned: PM, Tech

## Summary

Logged-in shoppers can manage their profile and saved shipping address, review past orders, and save favorite products to a wishlist they can move into the cart. Beginners often aren't ready to buy on the first visit, so the wishlist brings them back, and order history shows buyers what they ordered. It builds on accounts-auth (who the shopper is) and cart-checkout (cart and orders).
