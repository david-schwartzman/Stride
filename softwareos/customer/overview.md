# Stride — Overview

## Business

Stride is an online running store for runners. It sells running shoes, apparel and equipment through a small, curated catalog organized by the kind of running you do.

Its target audience is beginner runners getting ready for their first 5K/10K. Big retailers overwhelm beginners with thousands of options. Stride offers a small, hand-picked catalog with simple guidance ("good for your first 5K") so they can buy with confidence.

## Goals & Engagement

Build a complete e-commerce site from scratch over 4 weeks as a Moveo Academy capstone, using Spec-Driven Development with SoftwareOS.

Required pages:

1. Home
2. Catalog (search + filter by category)
3. Product detail
4. Cart
5. Checkout + confirmation (mock payment)
6. Sign-up / Login
7. Account / profile with order history
8. Favorites / wishlist

## Success Measures

- A beginner can go from landing to a confirmed order in under 3 minutes
- All 8 required pages work end to end, including empty and error states
- Every feature is traced to a spec with numbered acceptance criteria, built through PRs
